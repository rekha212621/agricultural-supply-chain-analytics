# KPI Reference

## 1. Farm Production

| KPI | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Production | Total agricultural production in tonnes |
| Total Cultivated Area | Total cultivated area in hectares |
| Average Yield | Average production yield in tonnes per hectare |

## 2. Distributor Supply

| KPI | Meaning | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Total Dispatched | Total quantity sent by distributors | Sum of dispatched quantity |
| Total Received | Total quantity received | Sum of received quantity |
| Total Wasted | Total recorded wastage | Sum of wasted quantity |
| Supply Efficiency (%) | Percentage of dispatched quantity received | Received ÷ Dispatched × 100 |
| Wastage (%) | Recorded wastage as a percentage of dispatched quantity | Wasted ÷ Dispatched × 100 |

## 3. Retail Sales

| KPI | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Quantity Sold | Total quantity of crops sold |
| Total Sales Revenue | Total recorded sales revenue in rupees |
| Average Selling Price | Average selling price per kilogram |
| Total Quantity Available | Total quantity available for sale |

## Notes

- The Farm Production dataset contains real agricultural data.
- Distributor Supply and Retail Sales use synthetic data created for demonstration.
- KPI values depend on the selected crop, district, date, and other filters.
- The exact units and formulas should match the measures configured in the Power BI report.
